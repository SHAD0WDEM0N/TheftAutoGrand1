Display & compatibility

Game resolution (F11) asks the original GTA DOS game to cycle through its available video modes. Use it during gameplay.

Crisp pixels and Smooth scaling only change how the browser enlarges the picture. They do not create new texture detail.

The player preserves GTA's 4:3 aspect ratio. Fullscreen fills as much of your monitor as possible without stretching the picture to 16:9.

Desktop Chrome or Edge is recommended. Mobile development is paused for now.
