Display & compatibility

Choose before launching GTA; switching requires ending and restarting the game.

Check GTA version reports the running DOS executable. Retail GTA may include GTA8.EXE and GTA24.EXE; higher-resolution modes are more likely to be available in the 24-bit version.

Screen mode (F11) sends GTA's documented video-mode key while the game runs. It may do nothing in certain game states or editions, and does not itself force 1080p or the maximum mode.

GTA saves its resolution preference in GTADATA/PLAYER_A.DAT. To keep a confirmed setting, export an updated private .jsdos backup after changing it. Never edit the original game's save data without a backup.

Enhanced Graphics — Phase 1 is part of this website, with no extra downloads or mod installation. Original, Enhanced Pixels, Vibrant Classic and CRT Classic switch instantly during gameplay. All modes keep sharp pixel scaling and 4:3 output.

Phase 1 adds lightweight colour/contrast treatments and optional subtle CRT scanlines. It does not change GTA textures, redraw sprites, implement xBR/Scale2x, or raise the game's native resolution. Those are future phases.

Aspect ratio is locked to 4:3, including fullscreen. On widescreen monitors, black side bars keep GTA from stretching to 16:9. Original VGA resolutions such as 320×200 are shown with classic CRT-style aspect correction.

Verify before Play checks the ISO or .jsdos archive structure and prepares the emulator code; it does not run GTA or precompile every graphics operation. Full gameplay compatibility is only confirmed when you start the game.

Save screenshot captures the game's native framebuffer as a local PNG for troubleshooting. A fullscreen monitor does not increase its native pixel dimensions.

The player maintains a 4:3 game viewport, with black bars where needed rather than stretching to 16:9.

Desktop Chrome or Edge is recommended. Mobile development is paused for now.
